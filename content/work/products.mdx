## Products

**MemoryLayer.** Local-first, MCP-native persistent memory for AI agents, with hybrid vector and BM25 retrieval. I built and launched it solo. A fuller write-up is coming with its next major version.

**ChefQuest.** A generative-AI recipe product built on Next.js and Supabase.

**Hush.** An Android privacy app built on Expo and a native VpnService. It blocks internet per app, shows which hosts each app contacts, and identifies and silences unwanted calls and SMS.

**Plantopia.** A gamified AI plant-care app with camera capture, weather data and growth timelines, built on Next.js, Supabase and a vision model.
