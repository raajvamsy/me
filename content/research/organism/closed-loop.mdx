## The question

Can the abilities from the foundations run inside one creature, all the time, and actually help it stay alive?

## What the code holds

About 95 new pre-registered predictions and a large set of modules in `creature/`, including causal intervention, experiment selection, belief revision, competing theories, embodied science and self-rejection of hypotheses.

## M13: science in a real body

| Card | Result |
|---|---|
| S1 | The embodied loop ran on every tick and survived all 4 claims |
| S2 | Knowledge changed behaviour: error 0.036 against 4.342 without it, t 11.73 |
| S3 | Knowledge survived a full reset from disk, t 18.18 |
| S4c | The registered energy metric showed no benefit, t 0.17 |
| S4c-R | A follow-up audit found the real benefit on water: margin 0.210 against 0.098, t 2.32 |

Survival was 14 of 20 lives with science against 8 of 20 without, but that number was unregistered, so I reported it as such and didn't substitute it for the verdict.

## What it taught

An audit showed where the behaviour really came from: the creature learned rates and maps, but the strategy menu, planner rules and replan rules were written by me. That was the honest reason to stop adding parts and start again from a core with no hand-written behaviour.
