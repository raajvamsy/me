## How this research works

Every experiment is a card: a prediction is committed before the run, along with a kill criterion, and the result is recorded as SURVIVE or DIE. A later card can repair a design, but it never re-runs or edits the card whose history it repairs.

The work moved through three stages. First it asked what the substrate could do at all. Then it asked whether those abilities survive inside a single living creature, and they mostly didn't. So it started again from a clean core built for one purpose: learning to stay alive in any body.

`lc`, `lc1` and `livingcreature` are clones of the same repository. The other `claude/*` branches are working copies of this same work.
