## The caregiver line

An LLM "parent" teaches the creature a word, but never controls its movement. The test is whether the word lasts after the parent leaves.

| Card | Test | Result |
|---|---|---|
| CG4 | Say FOOD at each meal | Missed the bar, 34 of 40 against 35 |
| CG5 | Replay tagged memories at night | Held. FOOD named over a fake word on 40 of 40 lives |
| CG7 | Copy that memory into a new body | Held on 40 of 40 lives |
| CG8 | Live Qwen as the parent | Failed. 2 of 8 lives, needing 6 |
| CG9 | Ground lessons in place and speech | Failed. 0 of 8 lives |

The scripted parent worked and the live model didn't, which separated the learning mechanism from the quality of the teaching.

## Populations

The FO cards bred populations on an interview-style score across survival, memory, invention and curiosity, trying different teacher designs and self-tuning.

## What it taught

Passing many separate batteries showed the mechanisms could work. It didn't show that they would work together in one life, and that became the next branch's question.
