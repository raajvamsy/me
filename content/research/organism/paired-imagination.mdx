## The question

One core, no scripted behaviour, three bodies: does it beat acting at random, never learning, and stopping learning halfway through life?

## What the code holds

| Module | Role |
|---|---|
| `boundary.py`, `constants.py` | The body boundary. A test forbids body imports or vocabulary in the core |
| `model.py`, `memory.py` | Five small networks and episodic memory, with calibrated uncertainty |
| `value.py`, `habit.py`, `need.py` | Need cost, TD value and a habit trained by learned advantage |
| `imagination.py`, `choice.py` | Compares candidate plans by simulating them, and commits to one |
| `episodic.py` | Fast recall of what really happened, combined with imagination by precision |
| `rehearse.py` | Teaches the habit from imagined rehearsal and confirmed choices |
| `genome.py` | Moves every constant into a genome passed at birth |

The core is about 1,800 lines. The bodies are separate: A (arena), B (day-cycle device), C (cyclic forager), and two speaking bodies, S and V.

## Results

| Gate | A: arena | B: device | C: forager |
|---|---|---|---|
| Predictions improve | No (2 of 5) | Yes (4 of 5) | Yes (4 of 5) |
| Learned beats newborn | No, t 0.73 | Yes, t 11.44 | Yes, t 1.79 |
| Less out of range than controls | No | Yes, t 17.7 and 15.5 | No, t 1.52 |
| Keeps adapting after a change | Not applicable | No, t −0.71 | No, t 1.46 |

## What failed

| Attempt | Lesson |
|---|---|
| Recency replay everywhere | Slow learners must interleave old and new |
| Surprise-prioritised replay | It ruins a general learner's accuracy |
| Distillation, early versions | Winner's curse and shared model error |
| Real-episode lessons only | Safe, but too little evidence: 1 to 7 credible lessons per life in the arena |
| Surprise-gated recency | Failed its frozen rule, t −0.32 in B and 1.46 in C. Removed |
| Curiosity, two designs | Reverted |

One real discovery came from replaying a failing life with read-only observers. After success, the habit's spread collapsed, so rehearsal stopped comparing options. The memory and the world model were both still right, which refuted my first explanation.

## Where it stands

The current work makes sleep a learned choice in place of a fixed replay schedule. Its runs were in progress when this page was written, so I don't report a result here.
