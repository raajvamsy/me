## The design

| Part | Job |
|---|---|
| Organism | Lives every tick, with needs, a habit and imagination |
| Language | Gemma 4, running only at decision points |
| Familiarity | One-shot recognition of names, using FunnelBrain |
| Memory | Episodes in MemoryLayer, recalled while thinking |

What the character says about the past must cite a recalled event. A memory of someone merely *saying* something doesn't count as proof.

The body learns what its world does from experience, but its steering is still partly hand-written, and it fails in this world without that steering. [The recorded life](/research/living-character/proof-of-concept) shows both.
