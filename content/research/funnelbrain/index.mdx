## The idea

Bytes become points in a space. Repeated experience digs funnels there, and each funnel acts as a concept. Prediction error deepens it, and sleep splits a funnel whose modes disagree, keeping a new cluster only if it pays for itself.

## The overall conclusion

On its own, this substrate produces collage at best. Real generation needs a real generative prior, and the useful role here is memory and conditioning around a frozen model. Its nested funnels went on to power [FFIC](/research/compression/ffic).
