## What it did

Split images by dominant colour, then per channel, into white and black masks, and into four shade bands per channel, with a static page for mixing the layers back together.

## Result

This was for looking at structure directly, and there's no compression number from it. Related ideas tested on the FFIC branch cost 10–24%, so slicing images into layers turned out to be a poor route.
