## The idea

A region isn't a new algorithm. It's the same wave circuit, specialised by what it receives, which rhythm it runs in, where it projects, and one read-out.

## Measured against a small LLM

| Property | Miniature brain | Small LLM (measured where stated) |
|---|---|---|
| Exposures to learn a fact | 1 | Pretraining, or in context |
| Retention across 5 domains | 100% | A tiny baseline kept 20% |
| Novel facts | Refused 100% by construction | The measured 1.5B also refused 6 of 6, but this isn't guaranteed |
| Transitive reasoning | 4 of 4, with a checkable proof | 4 of 4 in context, without a sound proof |
| Computing formulas | 4 of 4, and it refused an untaught one | 2 of 4, and it made up a value |
| Footprint | About 561 KB | Gigabytes |
| Generative breadth | Narrow, fixed grammar | Vast |

These are small hand-built tests, and the last row is the real trade-off.

## The honest mouth

The brain thinks, an LLM speaks, and `verify` checks each generated claim, so that only grounded claims ship. This idea carried over into MemoryLayer's verification.
