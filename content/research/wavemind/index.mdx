## The idea

Each concept is an oscillation with a frequency, phase and amplitude, and learning is a small set of online wave operations: cluster, create, merge and normalize. There is no training run and no backpropagation.

## The honest summary

It is strong at what it was built for, which is one-shot learning, retention and provable recall. It is weak at prediction and generation. Several results on these branches are negative, and I've kept them because they show exactly where the approach stops.
