## Scalper scanner

Replayed exactly as live, one 3-minute candle at a time, with fills on 1-minute candles and realistic fees and slippage.

| Measure | Result |
|---|---:|
| Average before costs | −0.05R |
| Average after costs | −0.66R (95% CI −0.69 to −0.63) |
| Random entries, same risk | −0.88R |
| With extra quality gates | −0.78R |
| Blocked by those gates | −0.64R |

The signals have no direction before costs. The median stop is so tight that costs take about 0.85R per trade. The quality gates, added from one screenshot, made things worse.

## Sweep, shift and gap

Rules were written before any code ran on any data.

| Bar | Result |
|---|---|
| Sample of at least 100 | Met |
| Lower bound above 0 | Not met, −0.40 |
| Above the random baseline | Not met |

**Verdict: no validated edge.** The 15-minute version was the least bad, consistent with fees mattering less on wider stops. As registered, there was no more tuning on this data.

Survivorship is a known caveat, because the pairs are today's most liquid.
